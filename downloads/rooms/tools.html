Tools

Jira, Confluence, Visio, Miro, Lucid, and Figma.

  • Jira, Confluence, Visio, Miro, Lucid, and Figma are places teams keep work. The decision still lives in a sentence a person can accept. Put the outcome and the test on the ticket. Put the decision, the date, and the owner on the page. A polished board with no limit is still a list.
  • Title: show the return reason. Description: the story and one Given-When-Then. Link: the business need identifier. Status moves to done only when that scenario passes.
  • The screenshot in the ticket is an illustration. The scenario is the requirement.
  • Jira. Atlassian's getting-started guide for Jira opened. Use it to keep work visible: items, states, and owners. The workflow should match the real approval path. Do not add states nobody will use.
  • Confluence. Confluence is Atlassian's space for longer pages. A requirement page needs an owner, a status, and a link to the backlog item. A page without a status looks current long after it is not.
  • Azure DevOps boards. Microsoft's Azure DevOps backlogs documentation opened. Boards are another place teams order work. The analysis habits are the same as in Jira: one backlog, clear states, and acceptance criteria on the item.
  • Visio. Microsoft's Visio support home opened. Visio is a diagramming product teams use for process and system drawings. The drawing is only as good as the labels. Export a copy stakeholders can open.
  • Lucid and BPMN. Lucid's BPMN tutorial opened. Use a shared diagram when more than one person must edit. Agree on a small stencil so the file does not become a clip-art board.
  • Miro. Miro's Kanban page opened. A board is useful in a workshop because people can move notes. After the workshop, copy the decisions into the system of record. A board that is the only copy will be lost.
  • Figma. Figma's home page opened. It is a collaborative design surface teams use for interfaces. Treat a frame as a prototype at a stated fidelity. Comments on a frame are not approved requirements until someone records them as such.
  • Power Platform and Dynamics. Microsoft's Power Platform ALM overview and the Dynamics 365 implementation guide both opened. They are relevant when a solution will be configured rather than only described. The business analyst still writes the need, the roles, and the acceptance checks. This is study context, not a claim of delivery experience.
