Business requirements outline

Why the change exists, for a sponsor.

  • Problem or opportunity in one paragraph.
  • Stakeholders and the decision maker.
  • Outcomes and how they will be measured.
  • Scope in and scope out.
  • Constraints, assumptions, and risks.
  • Recommendation, or a clear statement that more discovery is required.
