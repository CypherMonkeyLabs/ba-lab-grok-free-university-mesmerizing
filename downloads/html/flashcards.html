Flashcards

Term on the left, study meaning on the right.

  • Business analysis: The practice of understanding a need and shaping a valuable change.
  • Business analyst: A person who performs business analysis, whatever the job title.
  • Need: A problem or opportunity to be addressed.
  • Stakeholder: A person or group with a relationship to the change.
  • Requirement: A usable representation of a need, ready for further work.
  • Design: A usable representation of a solution.
  • Constraint: A limit on the solution that the team does not choose.
  • Assumption: A belief treated as true for now, still to be confirmed.
  • Scope: The boundary of what is in and out of the change.
  • Elicitation: Drawing out information from stakeholders and other sources.
  • Workshop: A facilitated session with a purpose and a record.
  • Baseline: An agreed version used for later comparison.
  • Traceability: A link from a need to requirements, designs, and tests.
  • RTM: A requirements traceability matrix: a table of those links.
  • UAT: User acceptance testing: a decision against agreed criteria.
  • User story: A short statement of role, capability, and outcome.
  • Acceptance criteria: The conditions that show a story or requirement is met.
  • Gherkin: A language for scenarios using Given, When, and Then.
  • BPMN: Business Process Model and Notation, published by OMG.
  • Gateway: A BPMN decision point that splits or joins flow.
  • UML: Unified Modeling Language, published by OMG.
  • ERD: A drawing of entities and the relationships between them.
  • C4: A model with system, container, component, and code views.
  • KPI: A key performance indicator tied to a decision.
  • HEART: Happiness, engagement, adoption, retention, and task success.
  • OKR: An objective plus measurable key results.
  • Product backlog: The ordered list of what is known to be needed in the product.
  • Increment: A concrete step toward the product goal, in Scrum.
  • Product owner: An accountability on the Scrum Team for the backlog and value.
  • RACI: A matrix of responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed.
  • SWOT: Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
  • ADKAR: Awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, and reinforcement.
  • Business case: A recommendation that shows why a course of action is worth it.
  • Fit-gap: A comparison of what a product already does and what the need still requires.
  • Process mining: Reconstructing a process from event logs.
  • Non-functional requirement: A quality attribute such as speed, security, or availability.
  • CBAP: Certified Business Analysis Professional, an IIBA credential.
  • ECBA: Entry Certificate in Business Analysis, an IIBA credential.
  • PMI-PBA: PMI's Professional in Business Analysis credential.
  • BACCM: The Business Analysis Core Concept Model in the BABOK Guide.
